Information We Collect

We adhere to data‑minimization principles and only gather data required to run step‑tracking functions, footprint record‑keeping, AI wellness assistant and overall service stabilization. The data collected varies slightly based on your device model and feature permissions you grant:

•                      Device‑Related Technical Data: Automatically gathered information covering device hardware model, operating system release version, advertising identifiers (IDFA for iOS / GAID for Android), public IP address, network connection status, system language and time‑zone configurations. This set of data helps us resolve compatibility bugs, analyze regional service access and defend against abnormal access behavior.

•                      Usage & Operational Log Data: Anonymous runtime records including application session timestamps, step‑tracking operation logs, footprint‑map interaction behaviors, feature access frequency, crash snapshots and error reports. All statistical information is processed without personal identifiers to optimize application performance and polish user experience.

•                      Voluntary User‑Input Text (AI Wellness Assistant Module): When you activate our built‑in AI wellness assistant to get walking‑related lifestyle advice, you may manually input text messages within the chat interface. We only collect and process the text content you actively type. We will not automatically pull your photos, location files, health‑record files or contact information without your separate permission.

How We Utilize Collected Information

All obtained data shall only be applied for the following legitimate service‑driven objectives:

•                      Deliver, run and maintain core application capabilities including step counting, footprint path recording and AI wellness consultation functions;

•                      Refine application operating efficiency, optimize interface display effects for different mobile devices, and improve overall user experience;

•                      Locate technical exceptions, fix software crashes, identify abnormal service usage and guarantee stable service operation;

•                      Handle your submitted feedback, functional suggestions and service‑related requests;

•                      Fulfill compliance obligations set by mobile application platforms and applicable local laws and regulations.

Third‑Party AI Assistant & Data Sharing Rules

Footprint Walk On integrates an external third‑party AI service to power its wellness assistant, delivering general lifestyle and walking‑fitness guidance for end‑users. Specific data transmission rules are defined as below:

  1.                 Scope of Transmitted Content: Only the text messages you voluntarily type inside the AI assistant chat box will be sent outward. We will never attach your real‑name information, identity credentials, location data, private medical records or image files during transmission.
  1.                 Authorized Data Recipient: Your chat text will be transferred to Gemini AI for semantic analysis, logical computation and generation of corresponding wellness replies.
  1.                 Restrictions on Third‑Party Data Usage: The transmitted chat content can only be used to generate real‑time replies for your current consultation session. The third‑party AI provider is forbidden to leverage such chat materials for targeted advertising, commercial marketing, user profiling or any other unrelated business scenarios.
  1.                 User Authorization and Independent Control: An explicit pop‑up consent is required before you can turn on the AI wellness assistant feature. You are allowed to disable this AI chat module at any time through Application Settings. Once disabled, text input will no longer be uploaded to the third‑party AI service, and AI‑driven consultation functions will stop working completely.

Circumstances for Information Disclosure

We will never sell, rent or trade your personal identifiable information for commercial marketing purposes. Your data may only be disclosed externally under the following compliant conditions:

•                      You have given clear, explicit authorization or consent for information disclosure;

•                      Disclosure is required by valid laws, regulatory requirements, judicial procedures or formal official government requests;

•                      Disclosure is necessary to safeguard our legitimate rights, property security, as well as the safety of other Application users and members of the public.

Data Security Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your stored and transmitted data from unauthorized access, information leakage, tampering and accidental loss. All data transmission channels adopt industry‑standard encryption protection. Chat texts sent to third‑party AI services are only used for real‑time reply generation and will not be stored persistently without justified reasons.

Children’s Privacy Statement

Footprint Walk On is oriented toward users aged 13 and older. We do not purposefully collect any personal data belonging to children under 13 years of age. If we find that personal information of minors under 13 has been obtained unintentionally, please reach out to our support email promptly so we can erase such data without delay.

Such content cannot replace professional physician diagnosis, formal medical treatment or authoritative medical advice. Whenever you face physical discomfort or health‑related concerns, please seek consultation from qualified medical practitioners.
